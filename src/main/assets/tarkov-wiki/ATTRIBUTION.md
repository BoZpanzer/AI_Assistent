The mirror credits RE3MR, Jindouz and xTycho and identifies the map images as
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
Additional author credits embedded in each image are retained. Customs credits
monkimonkimonk and Glory4lyfe. See provenance.json for original Wiki filenames,
mirror revision and SHA-256 integrity hashes for every image.

The mirror converted images to WebP and limited width to 2600 pixels; Factory
was cropped to its top 63%. JARVIS bundles those files unchanged. The maps remain
under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, separately from the application's code license.

These are community map snapshots, not guaranteed current layouts. Terminal is
not included. World-coordinate calibration has NOT been verified for these
images. Wiki variants have independent IDs, routes, goals and calibration to
avoid applying coordinates from a different image. The existing calibrated
Tarkov.dev SVG maps remain available separately.
